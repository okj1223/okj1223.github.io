My background connects environmental engineering, mechanical design, industrial automation, and robotics. That path shaped how I work: start from field constraints, define what can be measured, build the smallest useful system, and document the limits as carefully as the result.

## Current Work

- Humanoid robot data collection, teleoperation workflows, and dataset quality assurance
- ROS2-based autonomy, perception pipelines, and sim-to-real validation
- Industrial automation systems involving sensors, control, fluid handling, and safety engineering

## Engineering Approach

- **Own the boundary:** separate my direct contribution from team results, company IP, and illustrative examples.
- **Design for evidence:** prefer test records, real hardware media, diagrams, and reproducible artifacts over broad claims.
- **Work across layers:** connect mechanical design, sensing, control, software, and deployment constraints instead of optimizing one layer in isolation.
- **State limitations:** distinguish measured results, design targets, simulations, prototypes, and future work.

## Core Tools

- Software: Python, C/C++, ROS2, OpenCV, JavaScript, Docker, and Linux
- Systems: MQTT, Arduino, Raspberry Pi, instrumentation, control logic, and data logging
- Hardware: CAD, rapid prototyping, welding, fixtures, and machine integration
